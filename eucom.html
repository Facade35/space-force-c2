EUCOM Component Command Structure

Service components and functional components

Every force in Europe answers to two kinds of component commander. A service component commander runs one service's forces for the combatant commander and does that service's administrative work. A functional component commander directs forces of several services in one domain. At USEUCOM the same officer usually wears both hats: the service hat gives OPCON of their own service's forces, and the functional hat gives TACON of other services' forces made available for that domain.

Service component commander

USAREUR-AF · NAVEUR-NAVAF · USAFE-AFAFRICA · MARFOREUR/AF · SPACEFOREUR-AF

Exists
Always. One standing command per service under the combatant command.
Forces
Only their own service's forces assigned or attached to USEUCOM.
Operational
Normally OPCON of those forces, delegated by CDRUSEUCOM.
Administrative
ADCON for their military department: personnel, training, equipping, discipline, and service logistics. This stays with the service whatever joint hat the commander also wears.
Answers to
CDRUSEUCOM for operations. Their service chief and military department for administration.

Functional component commander

JFLCC · JFMCC · JFACC · JFSOCC · JFSCC

Exists
When the combatant commander designates one, for an operation or as a standing role. Its authority is whatever the establishing commander gives it.
Forces
Forces of any service made available for that domain or function (land, maritime, air, special operations, space).
Operational
Normally TACON: detailed direction of missions. Not organizational authority over other services' forces.
Administrative
None over other services' forces. Their ADCON stays with their own service component.
Who holds it
Usually the service component commander with most of the forces and the C2 capability for that domain, so one person wears two hats.
