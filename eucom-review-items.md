# EUCOM page: items still open

As of 22 Oct 2026. Each is also flagged "Verify" on the page (`eucom.html`).

## Command relationships
1. **JFSCC for USEUCOM.** Shown as the SPACEFOREUR-AF commander (dual-hatted as COMSPACEFOR), per your call. No public source names a USEUCOM JFSCC.
2. **JFLCC, JFMCC, JFACC, JFSOCC.** Shown as the usual dual hats (USAREUR-AF, NAVEUR-NAVAF, USAFE-AFAFRICA, SOCEUR). USEUCOM does not publish these designations.
3. **Functional TACON is shown as "when made available."** On the USSPACECOM page the CJFSCC holds TACON of all assigned units. Here I used the doctrinal default instead, because USEUCOM has five functional hats. Tell me if you want a blanket rule like the USSPACECOM one.
4. **Other services' space forces.** Your Marine rule (OPCON to the service component, TACON to the space component) is applied to every service's space elements in theater. No units are named publicly.
5. **Army air and missile defense (10th AAMDC, 52nd ADA Brigade).** Shown as TACON to the JFACC as area air defense commander, following doctrine. USEUCOM's actual arrangement is not published.
6. **Embarked MEU.** Older (2011) Navy descriptions put OPCON of TF 61/62 units with Sixth Fleet. The page shows OPCON with MARFOREUR/AF and TACON to the maritime commander. Confirm which you want.
7. **Cyber.** Shown as a USCYBERCOM support relationship through JFHQ-C (Air Force), from one July 2025 news report. FY26 legislation may have changed the alignment.

## Hats
8. **NATO hats shown:** SACEUR (CDRUSEUCOM), Commander, Allied Joint Force Command Naples (COMUSNAVEUR), Commander, Allied Air Command (COMUSAFE). Whether the SOCEUR commander also commands NATO's Special Operations Forces Command is not confirmed.
9. **USAFRICOM hats shown:** USAREUR-AF, NAVEUR-NAVAF, USAFE-AFAFRICA, MARFOREUR/AF, SPACEFOREUR-AF. SOCEUR is EUCOM only.

## Grades
10. Component and major command grades are typical public grades (O-10 for the Army, Navy and Air Force components, O-8 for MARFOREUR/AF and SOCEUR, O-7 for SPACEFOREUR-AF). I used O-6 where the grade is uncertain, including the USEUCOM J-directors, even though many are general or flag officers. Tell me if you want those left blank instead.
