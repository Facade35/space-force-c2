One commander, two chains

The Commander, Space Forces (COMSPACEFOR) of U.S. Space Forces–Space (S4S) answers to the Commander of U.S. Space Command for operations, and to the Chief of Space Operations (CSO) for administration and support of Space Force personnel.

Generate vs. employ

Combat Forces Command (CFC) organizes, trains and equips most Space Force operational units. When they are ready, combat formations are presented to S4S, which employs them for U.S. Space Command.

Same commander, extra hat

The S4S commander is also the Combined Joint Force Space Component Commander (CJFSCC), conducting joint space operations through five operations centers.
